A few words from my heart

Dear Sara

Some things deserve to be said gently.

Hey Sara

I’ve been thinking about sending this for three days. These past few days have been really hard for me and then I dreamed about you. In that dream you thought everything between us was a lie and that you were never enough for me. I know it was just a dream and maybe I got it all wrong but I woke up feeling like I couldn’t just ignore it.

I’m not telling you this to make you feel sorry for me. I just felt like I owed you an explanation. Life is unpredictable and maybe one day we won’t know anything about each other anymore. I didn’t want to leave things unsaid.

You were always enough Sara

What we had was real to me. I never cheated on you and I wasn’t pretending to care. I made mistakes and I know that but I hope the way things ended doesn’t erase every good moment we had.

I still remember the first time we studied together and how happy those little moments made me. I know how smart you are even when you doubt yourself. And I didn’t forget your birthday. I wanted to say something but I thought respecting your space mattered more.

I saw your Snapchat story today and you looked beautiful. I didn’t reply because I didn’t know if you’d want to hear from me.

I'm not your enemy

I know you might think I was manipulating you. I understand why you may see things differently but I wasn’t trying to play with your feelings. If I only wanted a reaction I could have reached out the next day. Instead I stayed away because I wanted to respect your boundaries. I’m not saying that makes me perfect. I just want you to know I did care.

Maybe you’re doing better now just like you told me the last time we spoke. I really hope you are. I remember when you felt like nobody truly knew you. I can’t say I know everything about you but I knew a side of you that meant so much to me.

You can tell me to fuck off or act like you never got this message or think I’m just some player. But honestly that’s not the Sara I remember. I remember someone who could look beyond the hurt and try to understand. Maybe I’m wrong and that’s okay too. I just wanted to be honest with you.

I’m not asking you to come back and I’m not expecting a reply. I hope you can read this when you’re alone and take your time with it. Maybe read it more than once and think about what I’m trying to say before you decide how you feel. I wrote these words for you and I hope they can stay between us.

I just felt I owed you these words. I hope life is gentle with you Sara. Take care of yourself.
